03 Studio

One person. One app at a time.

Most apps do too much. They grow features to justify a subscription, collect more than they need, and turn a small task into a thing you have to manage.

Margin Studio works the other way around. Pick one job, do it properly, ask for as little as possible — and leave the margins wide enough for the person using it to think.
