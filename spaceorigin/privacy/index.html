Space Origin: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Space Origin is a small arcade shooter for Android, made by Margin Studio. It has no accounts, no ads and no purchases. This page explains what the game keeps on your phone and the small amount of anonymous usage and crash information it sends to Google.

The short version

  • You never create an account or give us your name or email.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay events and crash reports to Google Firebase, so we can fix crashes and see which levels are too hard.
  • The game contains no advertising and does not use your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Space Origin is published by Margin Studio, an independent studio based in India. For anything in this policy, write to letscha8@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your level progress, stars, best score, difficulty, sound setting and whether you have seen the first-level hints. This is stored only on your device and is not uploaded. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes it.

What the game sends to us

The game uses two Google Firebase services. Both identify your device only by a random installation ID that the game generates for itself. It is not your name, email, phone number or advertising ID.

Firebase Analytics

  • Gameplay events: when a level starts, is cleared or is failed, when you tap replay, and when you leave a level for the menu. Each event carries the level number, the difficulty and, where relevant, your stars and score.
  • Standard information Firebase collects automatically: the installation ID, device model, Android version, app version, language, first-open and session times, and your approximate country, worked out from your IP address.

Firebase Crashlytics

  • Crash and error reports: the technical trace of what the game was doing, your device model, Android version and basic device state (such as free memory), a random Crashlytics installation ID, and the level and difficulty you were playing.

What we do not collect

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone, files, payment details, or the Android advertising ID. The game does not ask for permission to any of these.

Why we use it

Only to keep the game working and to improve it: finding and fixing crashes, and seeing where players get stuck or give up. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who it is shared with

The data goes to Google, which runs Firebase and processes it on our behalf. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. We may disclose information if the law requires it. To see how Google handles this data, read Privacy and Security in Firebase and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How long it is kept

  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.
  • Analytics event data is kept for at most 14 months.

Data stored on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Where it is processed

Google may process this data on servers in countries other than yours, including outside India. It is protected by Google’s own security practices and is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

There is no account to delete. Uninstalling the game stops all collection. Clearing its data or reinstalling it gives your device a new random installation ID, which breaks any link to earlier data.

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to letscha8@gmail.com. Because the data is anonymous, we can only find a particular device’s records if you can give us its Firebase installation ID, which the game does not display today. In practice, the retention periods above are how the data goes away. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we will honour them.

Children

Space Origin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting anything new, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches players.

Contact
